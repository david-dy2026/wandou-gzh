# 豌豆公众号改写

Use this skill when the user has an existing tutorial-like draft and wants it to become more attractive for WeChat Official Account or similar long-form Chinese content platforms.

The core job is not to make the prose flashy. The core job is to turn an information sequence into a readable article with a clear reader promise, a stronger opening, a sharper point of view, and a path that keeps people moving.

## Default Boundary

Respect the user's requested level of intervention.

- If they ask for diagnosis, do not rewrite the whole article.
- If they ask "how would you change it", give an editorial plan and example snippets.
- If they ask to rewrite, produce a complete revised draft.
- If the draft lives in a connected document, do not edit the source document unless the user explicitly asks for document changes.

## First Judgment

Before rewriting, identify what kind of draft it is:

- **Pure tutorial:** mainly steps, definitions, screenshots, procedures.
- **Experience tutorial:** a process plus the author's lived discovery or mistake.
- **Point-of-view tutorial:** a method framed by a claim about how people should think or work.
- **Tool demo:** a product or AI workflow demonstration.
- **Reading note:** ideas from a book, course, podcast, or article, with the author's interpretation.

Then decide what the article should become. Most plain tutorials become stronger when reframed as one of these:

- **Discovery article:** "I thought X was ordinary, then I found Y."
- **Mistake correction:** "Most people do X, but the useful move is Y."
- **Before/after transformation:** "I used to do X; now I do Y."
- **Method extraction:** "This is not just knowledge; it can become a reusable workflow/tool."
- **Case-led tutorial:** start from a concrete case, then unfold the method.

## Find The Real Hook

Do not assume the current title or first paragraph contains the best hook. Search the whole draft for the strongest material:

- A sentence with a bigger point of view.
- A surprising personal reaction.
- A before/after contrast.
- A common reader pain.
- A result or artifact the reader wants.
- A method that can be reused beyond the example.

If the strongest sentence appears near the end, consider moving its idea into the title, opening, and conclusion.

For AI/tutorial drafts, a common high-value hook is:

> This is not just using AI to summarize information; this is using AI to turn knowledge into a reusable tool or workflow.

Adapt the principle, not the wording.

## Opening Pattern

For公众号, avoid opening with generic context such as "今天给大家介绍", "最近看到", or long background setup unless the user's style requires it.

Prefer openings that combine:

1. **Ordinary scene:** what the author was casually doing.
2. **Unexpected pause:** why they stopped, noticed, or changed their mind.
3. **Hidden value:** what the reader will gain if they keep reading.

Useful shapes:

- "我本来只是随手看一眼，结果发现这里藏着一个更大的用法。"
- "正常情况下，这东西我会截图收藏，然后就忘了。但这次我多做了一步。"
- "很多人以为问题是 X，其实真正卡住的是 Y。"
- "这不是一个关于 X 的教程，而是一个关于 Y 的方法。"

Do not overuse dramatic language. The opening should feel like a real discovery, not manufactured suspense.

## Article Structure

For a full rewrite, a reliable structure is:

1. **Hook:** begin with the author's discovery, contradiction, or reader pain.
2. **Reframe:** name what this article is really about.
3. **Compressed context:** explain only the background needed to understand the method.
4. **Concrete demonstration:** show the original example, tool, model, or process.
5. **Author's case:** include a lived example or realistic use case early enough to build trust.
6. **Method extraction:** translate the example into reusable steps, criteria, or questions.
7. **Reader transfer:** show how the reader can apply it to their own work.
8. **Sharp ending:** return to the central claim in a memorable sentence.

Avoid equal-weight explanation. If the source draft lists many modules, features, or steps, compress the less important ones and enlarge the few that serve the article's central promise.

## Diagnosis Output

When the user asks for diagnosis, include:

- **Core judgment:** one sentence about why the draft feels plain or where its appeal is hidden.
- **Real hook:** the strongest idea that should carry the article.
- **Current structure:** describe how the draft currently flows.
- **Recommended structure:** describe the revised flow.
- **Specific changes:** what to move earlier, compress, delete, expand, or turn into examples.
- **Title directions:** 5-10 title options, grouped by angle when helpful.

Keep the advice concrete. Point to the draft's actual lines, sections, or ideas.

## Rewrite Output

When the user asks for a rewrite:

- Preserve the original substance and factual claims.
- Keep the author's first-person voice when present.
- Use clear Chinese prose suitable for公众号.
- Keep tutorial value intact, but make it feel like a guided discovery rather than a manual.
- Make section headings benefit-driven or curiosity-driven, not merely descriptive.
- Include images or placeholders only if the original draft depends on screenshots or diagrams.
- Do not invent unsupported results, metrics, authority, or personal experiences.

After the rewrite, briefly explain what changed:

- Opening angle
- Structural reframing
- Compressed or expanded parts
- Title recommendation

## Title Patterns

Generate titles from the article's real hook, not from the topic alone.

Useful patterns:

- "我本来只是 X，结果发现 Y"
- "别再只 X 了，真正有用的是 Y"
- "真正会用 AI 的人，不是 X，而是 Y"
- "把 X 变成 Y：这是我最近最想保留的一个方法"
- "一个小动作，让 X 从知识变成工具"
- "读完/看完 X 后，我让 AI 做了一个 Y"

Prefer specific verbs and concrete objects. Avoid empty adjectives like "高效", "重磅", "必看", unless the draft itself supports them.

## Quality Check

Before finishing, check:

- Can a reader understand why this matters within the first 3 paragraphs?
- Is there one central claim, not several competing claims?
- Does the tutorial still teach the promised method?
- Is the author's experience present enough to avoid sounding like copied knowledge?
- Are the title, opening, body, and ending serving the same idea?
- Is the article attractive without becoming misleading?
